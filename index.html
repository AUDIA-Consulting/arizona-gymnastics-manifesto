OUR VALUES 01 / 06

WOMEN

  • You empower the women around you, and create an environment where every woman feels respected and supported.
  • You inspire young girls in the community by demonstrating strength, confidence, and leadership.
  • You express your character through confident actions and walk through life self-assured.

OUR VALUES IN ACTION

WOMEN INVESTING
IN WOMEN

You inspire young girls in the community by demonstrating strength, confidence, and leadership.

OUR VALUES 02 / 06

BALANCE

  • You prioritize your responsibilities while understanding that gymnastics is something you do, not who you are.
  • You manage your emotions and communicate thoughtfully.
  • You make time for commitments that help you grow into a well-rounded person while remaining accountable.

OUR VALUES 03 / 06

FIERCE

  • You compete with confidence, courage, and purpose.
  • You respond to adversity with determination.
  • You bring energy and intensity into the gym every day and challenge yourself and your teammates to raise the standard.
  • You are bold enough to dream big and never stop working towards those goals.

OUR VALUES 04 / 06

SELFLESSNESS

  • You make the choice every day to show up for the betterment of your team.
  • You live by the “we over me” mindset.
  • You look for opportunities to support your teammates and community without being asked.

OUR VALUES 05 / 06

DEPENDABILITY

  • You represent the program with pride, and defend the culture when no one is watching.
  • You will embrace the difficult even when it is uncomfortable or unseen.
  • You follow through on your commitments.

OUR VALUES 06 / 06

EVERY TENTH

  • You express gratitude for every resource you are given.
  • You find the importance in the details.
  • You are intentional with your platform and maximize your time.

04 / IN THEIR WORDS

THE IMPACT
LASTS A LIFETIME.

Sophie Derr

Arizona Gymnastics

“I fought to stay on because I knew we needed the highest score possible.”

Read Sophie’s full story +

On day one of the regional semi-final competition, we started on bars. We had an unfortunate fall right before my turn to compete, so I knew my score needed to count. My routine didn’t go as I hoped. I had multiple moments where I almost came off the bar, but I fought to stay on because I knew we needed the highest score possible. While I initially was upset with my performance, John reminded me that I did my job by fighting to stay on the equipment, and that it was time to mentally reset for the rest of the meet. Through the next three events, our team continued to fight for every tenth and respond to adversity with determination. Because of our fight, we beat Arizona State and Georgia, by .075 of a tenth, to advance to the Sweet 16.

Karin Wurm

University of Arizona Sports Hall of Fame Inductee & Two-time NCAA All-American and four-time NCAA Academic All-American

“Arizona created a place where you could excel in athletics and academics while belonging to a sisterhood that becomes family.”

Read Karin’s full story +

Being an Arizona GymCat was, and continues to be, one of the most rewarding experiences of my life. Arizona created a place where you could excel in athletics and academics while belonging to a sisterhood that becomes family. At our recent 50-year program reunion, I heard women across five decades expressing that exact same sentiment.

That is such a rare and incredible thing about Arizona gymnastics. That special legacy doesn't happen by chance. Sustaining and elevating this program relies on the dedicated support of our letterwinners and donors.

Your support goes directly to future generations of GymCats and ensures they can compete at the highest level both on the mat and in life. I can testify that the impact does truly last a lifetime.

Elena Deets

2026 Arizona Gymnastics Ring of Honor Inductee & 2025 Big 12 Specialist of the Year

“The values I learned are what will continue to shape me long after my gymnastics career.”

Read Elena’s full story +

Arizona Gymnastics gave me so much more than the opportunity to fulfill my dream of competing at the Division I level. It challenged me to grow as an athlete, teammate, leader, and person.

I was fortunate to achieve great success both athletically and academically throughout my time in the program, but the values I learned are what will continue to shape me long after my gymnastics career. The support of Arizona Gymnastics donors makes experiences like mine possible.

Your generosity provides student-athletes with the resources, opportunities, and support they need to succeed both in the gym and beyond.

I am incredibly grateful to have been a part of this program, and I hope current/future GymCats can experience the same lifelong impact Arizona Gymnastics had on me. Bear Down and Go Cats!

Abby Martin

Arizona Gymnastics · 50-year reunion

“What we are building here is so much bigger than gymnastics, and the impact of being a GymCat lasts a lifetime.”

Read Abby’s full story +

This past year, over 100 Arizona Gymnastics alumni came back to celebrate our 50th anniversary. It was inspiring for me to see generations of GymCats all in the same place and hear about the impact this program had on their lives.

I got to see firsthand how Arizona Gymnastics helped shape these women into confident, secure, and thriving people far beyond their time in the gym. What we are building here is so much bigger than gymnastics, and the impact of being a GymCat lasts a lifetime.

05 / THE COACH’S STORY

THE ROUTINES END.
THE IMPACT DOESN’T.

John Court · Head Coach

John Court’s commitment to coaching reaches beyond the competition floor. He wants the women who come to Arizona to grow as people—to develop their character, discover what matters to them, and pursue their dreams with confidence.

That means guiding them through the demands of college gymnastics while making room for the lives they are building beyond it. It also means investing in the community together, helping each woman see how she can encourage, support, and open doors for others.
